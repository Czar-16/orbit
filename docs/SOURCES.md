# Source register

No external job sources are enabled in Phase 1. No job pages are fetched.

LinkedIn and Indeed are restricted to future user-driven intake: pasted URLs, pasted descriptions, or imported files/lists. No scraping, crawling, automated application actions, or private endpoints are permitted.

Before enabling any other source, record the official public API/feed, current terms URL, review date, allowed use, restrictions, and the resulting enable/disable decision here. Reachability alone does not establish permission.
